Privacy

Play Request is a tasting journal. We keep as little as we can, and you can delete it.

This page is for the Play Request website at platinum00001.github.io/play-request/. Last updated 23 November 2026 (America/New_York).

What stays on your device

Your tastings (which combos you tried, optional ratings, notes, and examples) are saved in this browser’s localStorage. A small first-party cookie named play_request_has_progress only records that you have some progress, so the site can greet you consistently. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers for ads.

What an account stores

If you sign in (email link, password, or later Google or Facebook), we store your login in Supabase Auth and, in this site’s own play_request database schema:

  • A profile (optional display name) created on first sign-in by the site — not by a trigger on the shared users table.
  • Your tastings, so the same path shows up on another phone.

Staff who have been listed as admins can see counts (how many members, how many tastings, popular combos), not your notes, from the staff page.

Sign-in providers

Google and Facebook buttons are on the account page. Until those providers are switched on in the project, they show as “coming soon.” When they are on, those companies will see that you are signing in to this app under their usual policies.

Data deletion

You can delete your Play Request data in any of these ways:

  • On this device: use the account page to clear or overwrite the local save, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Your account: sign in, open My account, and choose “Delete my Play Request data.” That removes your tastings and profile for this app and signs you out.
  • Guest forms, your login itself, or Google/Facebook sign-in data: email the contact address (or use the account page if you are signed in) with the subject “Delete my data” from the address you used. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email once a contact inbox is set.

Restaurant listings

Restaurant names, addresses and dishes are compiled from public sites and menus so you can go taste things. “From the menu” and “suggested” labels are explained on the board. Listing a restaurant is not a partnership unless we say so. Cape Island Café is featured because it is part of this network.

Contact

Questions: contact. If no address is shown yet, use the site owner through the All Sites Hub.
