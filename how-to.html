The rules

How to play

A short path is enough. You can reach the Peak in five tastings — or take the long way and collect them all.

The five tastes

Every flavor people talk about is built from some mix of sweet, sour, salty, bitter and umami. There are 31 non-empty combinations: 5 singles, 10 pairs, 10 trios, 5 quads, and the Peak — all five at once.

Unlocking

  1. All five single flavors are open from the start. Pick one. Try it. Mark it.
  2. A new combo opens when it extends any combo you have already tried by exactly one flavor.
  3. Example: try Sweet, and every pair that includes Sweet opens (Sweet + Sour, Sweet + Salty, and so on). You do not have to try Sour as a single first.
  4. Try Sweet + Sour, and every trio that is “Sweet + Sour + one more” opens. Keep going up.
  5. You never have to fill a whole level to climb. One path to the Peak is enough. Filling more squares is for badges, bragging rights, and discovering new dishes.

Logging a tasting

Open a square, optionally rate it 1–5, and add a note or the food you used. Progress is saved in this browser (and a small cookie that only remembers you have progress). Sign in if you want the same path on another phone.

Restaurants

Each combo lists dishes from Falmouth and Woods Hole. From the menu means we took the dish name from a published menu or official site (checked 23 November 2026, America/New_York). Suggested means we inferred a likely plate from the kitchen’s style — please confirm before you go. Menus change.

Related games

James already built two cousins of this idea: Flavor Quest, a Cape Cod restaurant adventure with a stricter flavor tree, and Flavor Escape, a story game about the same 31 combinations. Play Request is the simple tasting path — try one, open the next.
