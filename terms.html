Terms of use

Play Request is a free tasting journal and restaurant idea list. Please use it kindly.

Last updated 23 November 2026 (America/New_York).

The game

Play Request lets you track combinations of the five basic tastes and browse dish ideas at restaurants in Falmouth and Woods Hole, Massachusetts. It is provided as-is, for personal, non-commercial play, without a fee.

Accounts

You must be 13 or older to create an account. You are responsible for the email or sign-in method you use. Do not try to access someone else’s tastings or the staff page without permission.

Restaurant information

Menus, hours, prices and whether a place is open change. Dish mappings marked “suggested” are not a promise that the dish is on the menu today. Always confirm with the restaurant. A listing is not an endorsement or a paid partnership unless we say so. Please be a good guest if you visit.

Your notes

Don’t post illegal, hateful, or other people’s private information in notes. We may remove account data that breaks this.

Availability

The site may go down, change, or lose a local save if you clear your browser. Export a backup from the account page if you care about a long path.
